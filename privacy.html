Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: november 15, 2026

Welcome to Threaded! These Terms and Conditions outline the guidelines for browsing our website and submitting an order request. By using our website, you agree to the terms stated below.

1. About Threaded

Threaded is a T-shirt brand offering comfortable, versatile, and everyday clothing. Our website allows customers to explore our products and submit order requests through email.

2. Products and Pricing

We aim to provide accurate product descriptions, images, and prices. Product images are for reference and may vary slightly from the actual items. Please review product details carefully before placing an order.

3. Orders

To place an order, select your preferred product, size, and quantity, then click Order Now. This button opens your email application so you can send your order details to us.

Submitting an order request does not automatically confirm your purchase. We will review your request and contact you to confirm product availability and order details.

4. Payment and Delivery

Threaded does not process payments directly through this website. Payment methods, delivery arrangements, and any applicable fees will be discussed with the customer during order confirmation.

5. Cancellations and Changes

If you need to change or cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible. Requests will be reviewed based on the status of the order.

6. Returns and Exchanges

If you receive an incorrect, damaged, or defective item, please contact us with your order details so we can review the concern and discuss an appropriate resolution.

7. Acceptable Website Use

Visitors are expected to use the website responsibly and provide accurate information when submitting order requests. Activities that disrupt the website or interfere with other users are not permitted.

8. Privacy

Personal information submitted through our website or email should be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Please review that policy to understand how your information may be collected and used.

9. Changes to These Terms

Threaded may update these Terms and Conditions when necessary. Revised terms will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

For questions about these Terms and Conditions, please visit our Contact Us page to find our contact information.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At Threaded, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected when you visit our website or place an order, how it is used, and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an order request, we may collect information such as your name, email address, selected product, size, quantity, and other details you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, review order requests, confirm product availability, coordinate order details, and communicate with you about your purchase.

3. How Orders Are Processed

When you click Order Now, your email application opens so you can send us your order details. Please review your message before sending it. Your order request will be reviewed before we confirm the purchase.

4. Sharing of Information

Threaded does not intend to sell your personal information. We may use the information you provide to respond to your requests and manage your orders. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Data Protection and Retention

We will take reasonable steps to protect the personal information shared with us. Information should only be accessible to authorized individuals and retained for as long as necessary for its intended purpose or as required by applicable law.

6. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use basic technical features necessary for its operation. If cookies or analytics tools are used, this policy should be updated to explain what information they collect and how it is used.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, or to ask about its deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or website features change. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page to reach the Threaded team.
